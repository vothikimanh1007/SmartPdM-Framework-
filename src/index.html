Metric Interpretation

Health Index: The overall mechanical integrity grade. 85-100% is Optimal; 70-84% indicates early race degradation; Below 70% warrants critical maintenance.

Certainty Score: The model's internal confidence. High scores (>90%) indicate a distinct fault signature, while lower scores may suggest signal interference or unknown machine states.

Operational Logic

• Dynamic Routing: Switch to Context B for high-noise industrial shop floors to activate the Paderborn-robust weights.

• Normalization: The system applies Window-based Local Z-Score automatically to neutralize RPM variations and torque bias during real-time forecasting.
